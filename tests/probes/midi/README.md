# midi

MIDI files loaded as sequences: tempo, length, tracks and notes.

Language: Lua. Build: `pdc Source midi.pdx`.

Notes:

- `Source/song.mid` (a melody with a chord on channel 1) is listed in `midi.txt`, and `Source/song2.mid` (two notes on channel 1 and one on channel 10, the drum channel) in `midi2.txt`; `expected/sim_midi.txt` and `expected/sim_midi2.txt` are the Simulator's files.
