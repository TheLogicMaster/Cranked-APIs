## How this repository was made (AI use)

This repository was produced with substantial help from an AI system (Anthropic's Claude), working under the
direction of the project's maintainer. Most of the text, the probe programs, their comments and the tools were
written by the AI; the maintainer chose the scope, set the rules, and reviewed and accepted the result. The
behaviour it describes was established by running the probes in the Playdate SDK 3.1.1 Simulator and by analysis
of the device's software (OS 3.1.2). It has not been reviewed line by line, and it may contain mistakes. Check
anything important against the Simulator or a device.

Nothing here has been run on a physical Playdate yet. See README.md for how statements about the Simulator, the
device and hardware are marked.
